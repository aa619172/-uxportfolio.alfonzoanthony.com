01 / The opportunity

Calm should be easy to find.

The design brief imagines an approachable destination for meditation, guided breathwork, sleep, gratitude and mood reflection. Instead of treating every wellness activity as a separate destination, the product brings daily practices into one coherent experience.

Working design question: How might we help someone move from an overwhelming day into a small, purposeful ritual with minimal friction?

This is a proposed design challenge. Interviews, research findings and impact measurements have not yet been documented.
02 / Experience architecture

One experience. Four meaningful moments.

ArriveDiscoverPracticeReflectReturn
01 / ARRIVE

Home as a gentle invitation

Personalized welcome, daily thought, mood check-in and a clear route to a first session.

02 / DISCOVER

Find what you need

Explore meditation and wellness categories such as sleep, focus, anxiety relief and gratitude.

03 / PRACTICE

Stay with the moment

Guided breathing, meditation player controls, ambient soundscapes and sleep routines.

04 / REFLECT

Recognize small progress

Mood entries, gratitude prompts and a journal concept that supports reflection without pressure.

03 / Design exploration

A visual language built for ease.

The product brief specifies nature-inspired imagery, muted greens, calm blues, lavender and warm neutrals, rounded controls, smooth movement and mobile-first navigation. The intention is to reduce visual noise while providing clear paths through richer features.

Interface principles

Gentle hierarchy · Clear next action · Accessible contrast · Comfortable touch targets · Consistent navigation
